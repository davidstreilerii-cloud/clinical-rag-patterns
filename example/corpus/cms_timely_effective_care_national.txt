MEDICARE TIMELY AND EFFECTIVE CARE -- NATIONAL PROCESS MEASURES

Scope note: NATIONAL values. No facility is named.

These are PROCESS measures -- did the care happen, and how fast -- as opposed to
the outcome measures in the readmission and mortality tables. Units vary by
measure and are given per row:
  * OP_18* scores are minutes (median emergency department time).
  * SEP_*, SEV_SEP_*, OP_22, OP_23, OP_29, OP_31, IMM_3 and SAFE_USE_OF_OPIOIDS
    scores are proportions out of 100.
  * A measure ID ending _NINETIETH is the top-decile hospital value for the same
    measure, not a separate measure. It is the achievable benchmark.

The sepsis block is the one to read carefully. SEP_1 is the composite: it scores
as passed only if every element of the bundle was delivered in time. The component
rows -- severe sepsis 3-hour and 6-hour bundles, septic shock 3-hour and 6-hour
bundles -- score higher individually than the composite does, because the composite
fails whenever any one element fails. That arithmetic is why sepsis bundle
compliance is a documentation and timing problem before it is a clinical one.
