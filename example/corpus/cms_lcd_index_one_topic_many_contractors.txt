So a populated display_id marks a PROPOSED (draft) LCD and names the final LCD it corresponds to. It does not mark a superseded record. MEDICARE LOCAL COVERAGE DETERMINATIONS -- ONE POLICY TOPIC ACROSS CONTRACTORS
TOPIC: Botulinum Toxin Injections

Scope note: an LCD is issued by ONE Medicare Administrative Contractor and binds
only that contractor's jurisdiction. This is the opposite of a National Coverage
Determination. The table below is the whole point: one clinical topic, and
six separate FINAL LCDs held by five different contractor organisations, plus
eleven proposed/draft records attached to them.

Consequences that follow directly from this table:
  * An LCD identifier is meaningless without its contractor. 'The L number for
    botulinum toxin' is not a well-formed question -- there are six.
  * LCD numbers cannot be inferred, guessed, or pattern-matched. They are
    assigned, and adjacent numbers are unrelated policies.
  * Effective dates differ across contractors for the same topic, so 'the current
    policy' depends on where the beneficiary is. Here the revision effective dates
    are not all the same.
  * A draft LCD is not a policy. Eleven of the seventeen rows below are proposals.
    Quoting one as though it were in force would be wrong on its face.

A blank 'Draft of' column means the row IS a final LCD.

Reading the table: 6 of these 17 rows are final LCDs,
carrying their own effective dates. The other 11 are proposed LCDs; each names
the final LCD it is a draft of and has no effective dates, because a proposal has
no effect. A single final LCD can have more than one draft in the export -- L35170
has two (L39858 and L40353), which are successive proposal rounds.

Noridian appears against two final LCDs, L35170 and L35172. The export does not
say why in these fields; a contractor can administer more than one jurisdiction.
That explanation is not asserted by this data.

To read any of these policies you must go to the Medicare Coverage Database and
open the specific LCD for the specific jurisdiction. This corpus does not carry
their text.
