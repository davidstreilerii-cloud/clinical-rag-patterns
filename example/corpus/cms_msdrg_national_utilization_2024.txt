MEDICARE INPATIENT HOSPITALS -- NATIONAL TOTALS BY MS-DRG, DATA YEAR 2024

Scope note: these are NATIONAL aggregates across all Original Medicare Part A
IPPS discharges. No hospital, provider, or beneficiary is identified. Dollar
amounts are averages per discharge.

  * Tot_Dschrgs        - total Medicare discharges nationally for that DRG
  * Avg_Submtd_Cvrd_Chrg - average charge submitted by the hospital
  * Avg_Tot_Pymt_Amt   - average total payment (Medicare + beneficiary + third party)
  * Avg_Mdcr_Pymt_Amt  - average amount Medicare itself paid

Note on relative weights: this dataset reports observed discharges and payments.
It does NOT contain MS-DRG relative weights or geometric mean length of stay.
Those live in the IPPS Final Rule Table 5, which is a separate publication and is
not included in this corpus.

Read this the way a coder would: DRG 871 (septicemia with MCC) is the single
highest-volume inpatient Medicare DRG, and the with-MCC / with-CC / without-CC
triplets that appear repeatedly above are the severity tiers that documentation
specificity drives. The gap between the average submitted charge and the average
Medicare payment on any row is the chargemaster-to-payment spread, not a denial.
