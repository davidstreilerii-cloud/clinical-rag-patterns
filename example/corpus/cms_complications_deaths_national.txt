MEDICARE COMPLICATIONS AND DEATHS -- NATIONAL RATES

Scope note: NATIONAL rates across all reporting hospitals; no facility is named.

Units differ by measure family and are not interchangeable:
  * MORT_30_* and Hybrid_HWM  - deaths per 100 discharges (risk standardised)
  * COMP_HIP_KNEE             - complications per 100 discharges
  * PSI_03 .. PSI_15          - AHRQ Patient Safety Indicators, events per 1,000
                                eligible discharges (PSI_04 is per 1,000 at-risk
                                surgical discharges, which is why it reads in the
                                hundreds rather than in single digits)
  * PSI_90                    - a weighted composite index, not a rate. A value of
                                1.00 is the national reference point by construction.
