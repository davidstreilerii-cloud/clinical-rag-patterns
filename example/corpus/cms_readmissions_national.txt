MEDICARE UNPLANNED HOSPITAL VISITS -- NATIONAL RATES

Scope note: every number here is a NATIONAL rate computed by CMS across all
reporting hospitals. It is not any one hospital's score. The companion
hospital-level table (dataset 632h-zaca) has no national_rate column at all --
a per-facility 'score' column is the only rate it carries, and that column is
one facility's number, never a benchmark.

Readmission rates are expressed as readmissions per 100 discharges. The OP_32,
OP_35 and OP_36 measures use their own denominators, named in the measure text.

Notes on the blanks, taken from the published values rather than filled in:
  * The three EDAC_30 measures and OP_36 are published as 'Not Applicable'.
    EDAC is an excess-days measure, expressed as days per 100 discharges relative
    to expectation, so a single national rate is not defined for it. The row still
    carries hospital counts (fewer / average / more than expected).
  * Hybrid_HWR is published as 'Not Available' for this reporting period.

Background, not asserted by this dataset: the six READM_30 cohorts here -- heart
attack, heart failure, pneumonia, COPD, hip/knee replacement, CABG -- are the same
six the Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program penalises on. This table does not
say so; it is context for why these six and not others.
